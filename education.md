      <div class="tb-label">M.A. Thesis</div>
      <div class="tb-title">Assessment of Technological and Pedagogical Knowledge of Government School Teachers in E-Learning in Lalitpur Metropolitan City</div>
      <p>Employed a convergent parallel mixed-methods design combining quantitative survey data from 28 teachers (Science, Mathematics, English) with qualitative semi-structured interviews from ICT focal persons across 9 community schools.</p>
      <p style="margin-top:0.65rem;"><strong>Inspiration:</strong> The rapid integration of ICT tools and growth of e-learning platforms during COVID-19 motivated this study on teacher readiness to adopt educational technologies.</p>
      <p style="margin-top:0.65rem;"><strong>Key findings:</strong> Teachers demonstrate strong pedagogical skills but limited technological confidence. Effective digital education requires clear policy, continuous training, reliable infrastructure, and community engagement.</p>
    </div>

      <p>Analyzed COVID-19's impact on Nepal's climate governance and NDC commitments through desk reviews and Key Informant Interviews with nine national and international organisations in Kathmandu Valley. Found low-to-medium pandemic impact on funding, with anticipation of future financial constraints.</p>
